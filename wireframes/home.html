Who we're for

Wineries

Family and mid-scale wineries: want to start exporting or enter new markets, don't have an in-house international sales team, are looking for a representative at trade fairs abroad or an importer. For them, VH Selection is an international export partner.

Importers and distributors

Importers, national distributors, wine retail chains, HoReCa distributors, wine buyers — the company is a source of vetted producers.
